# Build-a-closing-strategy-for-Bay-Area-technical-candidates:

# 1.	Start Closing From the First Call:
What you do:
•	Ask about motivators (impact, tech stack, team, growth, stability).
•	Ask about deal breakers (comp, location, remote, role scope).
•	Ask about timeline (other interviews, expected offers).
•	Ask about decision criteria (“What will matter most when choosing?”).
Why it works:
You’re not selling — you’re learning what to sell later

# 2.	Build a Personalized Value Narrative:

Once you know what matters to them, tailor your pitch.
Examples:
•	If they care about impact: highlight ownership, roadmap, and visibility.
•	If they care about tech: highlight architecture, research, innovation.
•	If they care about career growth: highlight leveling path and mentorship.
•	If they care about stability: highlight funding, leadership, and runway.
Bay Area candidates respond to:
•	Real problems they’ll solve
•	Real people they’ll work with
•	Real growth opportunities
Not generic selling.

# 3.	Keep the Process Fast and Predictable:

Speed is a closing strategy.
What you do:
•	Pre book interviews
•	Push for same week loops
•	Keep candidates warm between stages
•	Share timelines proactively
Why it works:
Slow processes lose candidates to faster companies.

# 4.	Use Hiring Managers as Your Secret Weapon:
Closing tactics:
•	HM 1:1 calls
•	Team meet and greets
•	Architecture deep dives
•	“Ask me anything” sessions

# 5.	Handle Compensation Transparently:
•	Share ranges early
•	Ask for competing offers respectfully
•	Explain equity, refreshers, and bonus structure
•	Highlight long term value, not just base

# 6.	Close With Confidence, Not Pressure
Use this structure:
•	Reconfirm motivators
•	Show how the role meets them
•	Address concerns
•	Present the offer clearly
•	Give space, but stay close

# 7.	Stay Close Until Signature:
•	Daily check ins
•	Offer breakdown calls
•	HM follow ups
•	Answer questions fast
•	Help them compare options (without pressure)
